Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Introduction

Comtom Engineering ("we", "us") operates https://comtom.engineering (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information collected through the Site. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, and the contents of a message sent through the contact form — in order to respond to your inquiry. Contact submissions are transmitted to our project-management tooling. The Site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (see "Analytics" below). We do not use tracking pixels or ad-tech cookies beyond what the analytics provider requires.

Analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics (via Google tag / gtag) to measure visits, pageviews, and referral sources. Google may collect your IP address and other standard request parameters. You can opt out of cookie-based analytics collection through Google’s ads settings; analytics collection itself continues unless your browser blocks the script.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and to scope any resulting engagement. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties other than the hosting and analytics providers described in this policy.

Data Retention & Security

We retain contact submissions as long as they are relevant to an ongoing or potential engagement. We use industry-standard safeguards in transit and at rest. No payment card or other financial data is ever collected on the Site.

Your Choices

You may request deletion of any personal information we hold by emailing us at the contact address on the Site, or by submitting a message through the contact form. Where processing is based on a request you made, we will delete the data within a reasonable time.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

Changes & Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above reflects the current revision. For questions, contact us via the contact form on the Site.
